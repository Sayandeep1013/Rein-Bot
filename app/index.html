How a round works

  1. 01

    A frame appears

    One still from an opening. Then a second at seven seconds, a third at fourteen. Every frame is checked by OCR first, so the show never spells out its own name.

  2. 02

    Everybody types

    Free text, not multiple choice. Typos, romaji, English, Japanese, fan abbreviations — the matcher takes all of them.

  3. 03

    First one wins

    Fastest correct answer takes the round and the clock stops dead. Up to 200 points for an instant call, 100 if you scrape in at the buzzer.
