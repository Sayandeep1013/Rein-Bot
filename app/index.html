How a round works

    
    
      
  1. 
        
    01_frame
    
        
    
          
    A frame appears
    
          
    One still from the opening. A second at seven seconds, a third at fourteen.
             Every frame is OCR-checked first, so the show never spells out its own name.
    
        
    
      
    2. 
      
  2. 
        
    02_type
    
        
    
          
    Everybody types
    
          
    Free text, not multiple choice. Typos, romaji, English, Japanese, fan
             abbreviations — the matcher takes all of them.
    
        
    
      
    3. 
      
  3. 
        
    03_win
    
        
    
          
    First one wins
    
          
    Fastest correct answer takes the round and the clock stops dead. 200 points
             for an instant call, 100 if you scrape in at the buzzer.
