Notes for the pipeline author:

- **Parent-franchise rule applied** (per B-11): sequels folded into their parent
  (Shippuuden kept separate from Naruto only because its OPs are culturally distinct;
  same for Brotherhood vs the 2003 series). Seasons are *not* separate entries.
- Movies are included (45–47) even though AnimeThemes coverage is thinner; expect some
  to yield zero usable clips and be skipped — that is the filter working as designed.
- Native spellings follow AnimeThemes/Japanese release conventions; `question_titles`
  stores them verbatim through `normalise_title()` at ingest, so fullwidth punctuation
  (e.g. 君の名は。's ideographic full stop) is handled by normalisation, proven in the
  migration execution tests.
- Difficulty inputs (`doc/DATA-MODEL.md` §5) derive from year/format/theme_type at ingest;
  this list deliberately spans 1989–2023 so every difficulty band has candidates.
